### What is a Skill
A Skill is a collection of **proactive knowledge** derived from data analysis.
This knowledge is used to make Architectural projects innovative, highly performant, and creative.

When a designer chooses a building concept generated by the system, its skills travel with that building into the designer's own workflow and platforms (e.g., Revit, Grasshopper, Forma, QGIS). Rather than sitting as passive documentation, each skill actively surfaces the right warning, creative prompt, study, or guidance at the moment a design decision is being made—using different tools and communication methods suited to its domain.

Those skills are generated by the three engines, each applying its overarching cognitive theme ([`engine_charter.md`](./engine_charter.md)):
- **Crystals Engine (Development — building around, in, or at POIs):** [`crystal_engine_skill_definition.md`](./crystal_engine_skill_definition.md)
- **Corals Engine (Coordination — using elements better together / introducing beneficial relationships):** [`corals_engine_skill_definition.md`](./corals_engine_skill_definition.md)
- **Cells Engine (Adaptation — unlocking an element's full potential by adapting it into something new/better):** [`cells_engine_skill_definition.md`](./cells_engine_skill_definition.md)

> **Stage C Execution Workflow:** For the complete on-demand workflow governing how a project selected from the NEST dashboard (`nest_dashboard.html`) generates its **6–9 `skill_name.json` files** (default **9**: `3 Crystals` + `3 Corals` + `3 Cells`, or 6–9 dynamically weighted toward the Lead Engine(s)) and updates the dashboard UI, see [`skill_generation_workflow.md`](./skill_generation_workflow.md).

The output skill should be in the form of a JSON file (`skill_name.json`).

### What is a Skill Made Of

### 1. Definition (`text`)
An overview of the skill's knowledge base and what its target is.

### 2. Types of Skills (Parent Engine)
- **Expected Data Type:** `enum (text)` — `Crystals Engine` | `Cells Engine` | `Corals Engine`

### 3. Trigger (`array of text`)
*When should this skill wake up in the designer's workflow?*
Conditions, design phases, modeling actions, or site features that activate the skill (e.g., `"massing orientation edit"`, `"cantilever span > 4m"`, `"site grading near existing cave"`).

### 4. Action (`object`)
*What does the skill proactively deliver to the designer?*
- **type** (`enum (text)` — `Warning` | `Prompt` | `Study` | `Guidance` | `Alternative`): The form of proactive intervention.
- **message** (`text`): The concise, actionable insight, warning, or creative question surfaced to the designer.

### 5. Attributes (`object`)
**What they are:** The **characteristics of the skill itself**—describing what kind of skill it is, how reliable it is, how it grows, what it connects to, what state it is in, what it remembers, and how it responds to change.

While `definition`, `parent_engine`, `trigger`, and `action` are universal across all skills (defining *what* the skill does and *when* it speaks up), `attributes` are engine-specific because each engine generates skills with a different nature:
- **Crystals Engine — Development** ([`crystal_engine_skill_definition.md`](./crystal_engine_skill_definition.md)) — Structured analytical & geometric rules for building around, in, or at POIs (`Scale`, `Facets`, `Axes`, `Density`, `Growth`, `Field`, `State`, `Memory`, `Bonds`, `Resilience`).
- **Corals Engine — Coordination** ([`corals_engine_skill_definition.md`](./corals_engine_skill_definition.md)) — Multi-element coordination, symbiosis & beneficial-relationship characteristics (`Resources`, `Branches`, `Symbiosis`, `Evaluation`, `State`, `Adaptation`, `Archive`).
- **Cells Engine — Adaptation** ([`cells_engine_skill_definition.md`](./cells_engine_skill_definition.md)) — Element-transformative, evidence-tracked adaptation characteristics (`Host Element`, `Latent Potential`, `Adaptation Pathway`, `Gene Refs`, `Novelty`, `Confidence`, `Reinterpretation Fallback`, `State`, `Evolution Memory`).
